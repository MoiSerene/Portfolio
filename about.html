I'm a Product Designer working at the intersection of AI, complex systems, and human collaboration. I specialize in AI-native products and complex systems, helping teams transform emerging technologies into intuitive experiences that create meaningful impact.

About

I enjoy tackling problems where technology, people, and workflows come together—transforming sophisticated AI capabilities into experiences that feel intuitive, trustworthy, and genuinely useful.

Over the past few years, I've designed enterprise AI products, researched knowledge-driven collaboration, and partnered closely with engineers, researchers, and product teams to shape AI-native experiences. Whether I'm facilitating discovery workshops, mapping complex workflows, or rapidly prototyping new ideas with AI, my goal remains the same: understand the bigger picture before designing the details.

I naturally think in systems rather than individual screens. I enjoy connecting perspectives across design, engineering, and business to build shared understanding, especially when navigating ambiguous or technically complex problems. I believe the best product decisions emerge when diverse expertise comes together around a common understanding of users.

What motivates me most is creating meaningful impact through technology. I'm fascinated by emerging technologies—not simply because they're new, but because they have the potential to fundamentally improve how people work, learn, and collaborate. As AI becomes increasingly capable, I believe designers have a responsibility to ensure these systems are not only powerful, but also understandable, transparent, and beneficial for the people who rely on them.
